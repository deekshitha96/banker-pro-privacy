Banker Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short

Banker Pro is a toolkit of loan calculators, everyday calculators, a document scanner, notes and games. Everything you type, scan or save stays on your phone. We do not run any server, we do not ask you to sign in, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data. The only outside service that sees anything about your device is Google, for the banner ad (see Advertising).

Data stored on your phone

  • Calculator inputs, loan templates, income and expense entries, notes and game progress.
  • Your name and profile photo, if you add them in Settings, and the general terms you edit for Key Fact Statements and sanction letters.
  • Scanned documents and paintings you choose to save.

This data is kept only in the app's storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Reports you share (PDF or Word) go only where you send them.

Permissions we use

  • Camera: to scan documents. Pictures are processed on your phone.
  • Photos and files: to pick a profile photo or document, and to save scans and paintings.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock: only if you turn on phone lock. The check is done by your phone; the app never sees your fingerprint or face.
  • Internet: for live currency rates, checking app updates and the banner ad (see below).

Online services

  • Currency rates: the Currency and Remittance tools fetch exchange rates from the free Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev). Only the currencies you ask for are sent; no personal details.
  • Update check: the app asks Google Play or the Apple App Store whether a newer version exists.
  • Text recognition: reading text from scans uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone.
  • Suggestions: "Send a suggestion" opens your own email app. We only see what you choose to send.

Advertising

Banker Pro shows one small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, below the menu bar, through Google AdMob. Ads are never shown inside pages or games, and they are limited to family-safe (G-rated) content. To show and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information, as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In Europe and the UK, Google asks for your consent first. On iPhone, ads are not personalised to you. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your phone's settings. None of the figures, notes or scans you enter in the app are shared with Google.

Children

Banker Pro is meant for adults working in banking and finance. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For any question, write to deebankerpro@gmail.com.
